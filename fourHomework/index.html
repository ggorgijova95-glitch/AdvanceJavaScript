Product List

Create a Constructor function for product. Each product has property: name, category (string), hasDiscount (bool) and price. Create an array of 15 products. Now answer the following requirements: * Find all products with price greater than 20. * Get the names of all products of Category Food, that are on discount. * Get the price of all products that are on discount. * Find the name and price of all products with name starting with a vowel, that are not on discount.
